# 1. WHAT THE LIBRARY SUPPORTS FOR THIS CUT

The strongest usable foundation is a gradual energy deficit, resistance training, adequate protein, and a food pattern he can adhere to; the athlete review reports protein at 1.6–2.4 g/kg/day during weight loss, while the obesity consensus says no single dietary pattern is universally superior and emphasizes adherence. [A \| 07_supplements/general_ods_position_stands \| 10.1007/s40279-025-02285-4] [A \| 12_population_AA \| 10.1002/jcsm.70167]

The retrieved athlete guidance places protein across four to six feedings and commonly reports 20–40 g or about 0.4–0.5 g/kg per meal. [A \| 07_supplements/general_ods_position_stands \| 10.1007/s40279-025-02285-4]

Tirzepatide can markedly reduce modeled energy intake, making diet quality and balanced intake important, while gastrointestinal events such as nausea, diarrhea, and constipation are common. [B \| 12_population_AA \| 10.1016/j.obpill.2026.100248] [A \| 05_fat_loss_drugs/tirzepatide_incretins \| 10.1503/cmaj.250502]

Carbohydrate should be adjusted to training demand rather than fixed at one number, but the library does not establish a precise pre-run or pre-lift dose for him. [A \| 07_supplements/general_ods_position_stands \| 10.1007/s40279-025-02285-4] [B \| 07_supplements/supplement_timing \| 10.1002/ejsc.12274]

The library does not settle an individual calorie target, a fiber gram target, a superior single food, a cheat-day effect size, or a personalized vitamin-D, omega-3, or magnesium dose.

# 4. LOW-PLANNING DEFAULT DAY

**PLANNING DEFAULT — repeatable workday; all food quantities and protein totals are label-checked planning estimates.**

- **Creatine:** **PLANNING AMOUNT / LOCKED USER CHOICE:** mix **5 g creatine monohydrate** into the post-training drink or take it with dinner on a non-training day.
- **Tirzepatide logistics:** **PLANNING DEFAULT:** use the clock rather than hunger to start each eating event; if a full plate is impractical, eat the protein component first and save the remaining food for the next scheduled feeding.
