# WEEK OPERATING PLAN

This date-agnostic operating week is for a Black male, age 26, in Dallas, cutting from about 230 lb to 200 lb in Phase A, then 190 lb in Phase B, using three progressive lifting days, running as primary cardio, and biking for easy volume or substitution. Start with today’s card—even Thursday—and continue forward; missed sessions never cancel daily light, protein, creatine, hydration, steps, easy movement, or the sleep window and are never stacked onto the next day.

# LOCKED NUMBERS

- Wake: **04:30 every day**; outdoor light is **04:35–04:50**.
- Bed: wind-down begins **19:15**; lights out is **20:15 every day**.
- Time in bed: **8.25 hours**; no weekend sleep-in or sleep-restriction block.
- Work: **Monday–Thursday, 06:00–16:00**; Friday–Sunday are off.
- Training: default start **17:00** and default duration **75 minutes**, unless the day card specifies a shorter session.
- Active cut: **Phase A, about 230 lb → 200 lb**. Phase B is 200 lb → 190 lb only after Phase A is stable for 2–3 weeks and lifts are not crashing.
- Protein: Phase A goal **200 lb = 90.7 kg**; **90.7 kg × 1.8 g/kg = 163 g**, rounded to **165 g/day**. Use four timed feedings of 40–45 g, with breakfast at least 40 g. Phase A floor is 145 g and heavy-lower/high-mileage cap is 200 g; this week’s printed target remains 165 g.
- Creatine: **5 g every day**, no loading; immediately post-session on training days and with dinner on a full rest-from-loading day.
- Caffeine: optional; last dose is **11:15**. No evening caffeinated pre-workout.
- Steps: default floor **10,000 every day**; Saturday follows the long-run exception and Sunday is **8,000–10,000 easy steps**.
- Heart rate: Tanaka only—**208 − (0.7 × 26) = 189.8**, rounded to **HRmax 190**. Zone 2 is **114–133 bpm**.
- Zone 2 governors: full-sentence talk test first, sustainable nasal breathing second, and 114–133 bpm as a check. Talk test wins when they disagree; the same rules govern running and biking.
- Hydration: **3–4 L/day baseline**, with additional fluid for sweat and more sodium from food in Dallas heat.

# RED-S AND PHASE RULES

- Remain in **Phase A** until the weekly average is near 200 lb, that level has been stable for **2–3 weeks**, and squat/row/bench performance is not crashing. One 200 lb weigh-in does not start Phase B.
- Phase A uses **165 g protein daily**, with a 145 g absolute poor-appetite floor and up to 200 g only on a justified heavy-lower/high-mileage day. Do not cut protein to accelerate loss.
- Phase B targets 190 lb and uses **155 g protein daily**, with a 138 g floor and 190 g high-mileage/heavy-lower cap. Change cards to 155 only when Phase B actually begins.
- Keep average loss around **0.5–1.0% bodyweight per week**. If average weight falls faster than about 2 lb/week for two weeks, add **150–200 kcal/day** rather than adding cardio.
