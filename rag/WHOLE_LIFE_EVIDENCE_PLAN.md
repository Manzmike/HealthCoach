# 0. PURPOSE AND DECISION LANGUAGE

This module evaluates the requested food, home, spiritual, recovery, “detox,” light, frequency, and traditional-practice ideas without allowing a selected item to become an automatic recommendation. It is an evidence-and-safety layer over the existing operating week. It does not change tirzepatide, prescribe IV fluids, diagnose thyroid disease, or turn an animal experiment into proof of human benefit.

Verdicts mean:

- **KEEP / ADD:** useful enough and compatible with the existing plan.
- **OPTIONAL:** reasonable preference or a limited-evidence experiment that must not displace the foundations.
- **CONDITION-SPECIFIC:** potentially relevant only when a symptom, diagnosis, lab, or sport demand matches the evidence.
- **CLINICIAN-ONLY:** evaluation or delivery requires a licensed clinician; no home protocol follows.
- **SKIP / DO NOT USE:** unsupported for the claimed purpose, unsafe for self-use, or both.
- **PLANNING DEFAULT:** logistics, not a physiological claim.

## Evidence ladder

1. Current public-health guidance, systematic reviews, and convergent controlled human trials control decisions.
2. One small human trial can justify **OPTIONAL/monitor**, not “proven.” Biomarker improvement is not automatically better performance, longer life, or disease treatment.
3. Animal or cell work is a **mechanism lead only**. It can move an item from “unknown” to “watchlist,” but never to a human dose, an ADD, or a replacement for care.
4. Testimonials, before/after photos, mechanistic diagrams, “ancient,” “frequency,” “detox,” and patent status are not efficacy evidence.
5. Any safety signal, interaction, contamination problem, or medical-delivery requirement can override interesting efficacy signals.

# 3. WATER QUALITY AND SOURCING

Dallas Water Utilities’ 2025 Consumer Confidence Report says the municipal system met or exceeded applicable state and federal requirements and lists detected contaminants. That report—not a pitcher advertisement—is the starting point. [City of Dallas 2025 Water Quality Report](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/waterutilities/PublishingImages/pages/water_quality_reports/2025%20Water%20Quality%20Report.pdf) EPA explains how to use Consumer Confidence Reports. [EPA CCR guide](https://www.epa.gov/ccr)

## Decision path

1. Identify the actual address/provider and read its current CCR. Apartment residents should also ask management about building age, recent plumbing work, and any building-specific testing; a city report does not test the final faucet.
2. Decide the problem: taste/chlorine, a specific health contaminant, hardness/scale, or emergency reliability. Do not buy “alkaline,” “structured,” “EZ,” or “high-vibration” water before naming the problem.
3. Select a filter certified for the exact reduction claim. NSF certification directories let buyers verify models rather than trust a logo screenshot. [NSF product certification](https://www.nsf.org/nutrition-wellness/product-and-ingredient-certification)
4. Verify the model number in the certifier’s live database, replacement interval, cartridge cost, flow rate, and whether performance requires professional installation.
5. Retest only when a building-specific concern, private well, official notice, or clinician/public-health reason makes it useful. A home “TDS” meter does not identify lead, pathogens, PFAS, or health safety.

| Setting | Good default | Upgrade only when justified | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apartment | Cold tap water after normal flushing; certified countertop/pitcher filter if taste or a matched reduction claim warrants it; leak tray under any device | Under-sink system only with landlord approval, shutoff access, and leak protection | Unapproved plumbing changes, permanent RO waste line, mineral-drop stacks, storing huge emergency jugs beyond floor/storage limits |
| House on municipal water | Same CCR-first method; certified point-of-use filter | Whole-house equipment only after testing identifies a whole-house problem; use licensed plumbing/electrical work | Treating hardness as toxicity; buying filtration from a salesperson’s unverified home demo |
| House on private well | **Different gate:** periodic testing through state/local public-health guidance is necessary because no municipal CCR covers the well | Treatment matched to laboratory results | Assuming clear/tasty water is safe |

**Minerals:** label mineral content may affect taste and dietary totals, but it does not make premium bottled water medically superior. Safe, accessible water that supports the established hydration plan wins. “EZ/structured water” remains a marketing claim unless a reproducible human clinical outcome and product verification appear.

## PLANNING DEFAULT — graded knee accessory block

This is for a pain-free user and is not rehabilitation. Add **one 8–10 minute block twice weekly**, preferably before Monday lower lifting and during Sunday mobility: 3–5 minutes easy backward walking on a clear flat surface, 2 × 12–15 tibialis raises, 2 × 10–15 calf raises, and 2 × 6–8 supported split squats per side at a comfortable range. Progress range before load. Stop and seek assessment for swelling, locking, giving way, sharp pain, gait change, or worsening symptoms the next day. Do not turn Tuesday/Thursday easy aerobic sessions into extra heavy leg days.

# 6. FAITH, BIBLE, AND THE HISTORICAL JESUS

This module keeps three questions separate:

1. **Historical:** what can standard historical methods infer about Jesus of Nazareth and the early movement?
2. **Textual/theological:** what do the New Testament and Christian tradition claim about who Jesus is?
3. **Spiritual practice:** how will prayer, Scripture, worship, and community shape daily life?

They overlap, but historical evidence does not experimentally prove divinity or resurrection; those are theological claims received in faith. Conversely, saying a claim is theological does not mean the underlying texts or historical people are imaginary.

## Primary-source evidence map

| Source | What it establishes | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, especially 15:1–11 | An early Christian witness records a received proclamation of Jesus’ death, burial, resurrection, named appearances, and Paul’s own experience. USCCB notes the formula is older than Paul’s wording. [USCCB 1 Corinthians 15](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1corinthians/15) | It is an insider Christian source and theological testimony, not a neutral modern report. |
| Synoptic Gospels and John | Multiple ancient narrative witnesses to Jesus’ ministry, teaching, death, and claimed resurrection. Catholic official teaching calls the Gospels the principal witness to Jesus’ life and teaching. [Vatican *Dei Verbum* 18–19](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_en.html) | They are theological ancient biographies written for communities; genre, authorship, sources, and differences need interpretation. |
| Josephus, *Antiquities* 20.9.1 | A first-century Jewish historian refers to James as the brother of “Jesus who was called Christ.” [Josephus text](https://openscriptorium.com/read/josephus/027-antiquities-book-20) | Josephus’ longer passage in Book 18 contains disputed/interpolated wording. Do not present every received sentence as equally secure. |
| Tacitus, *Annals* 15.44 | A non-Christian Roman historian reports that “Christus” was executed under Pontius Pilate during Tiberius’ reign and that Christians existed in Rome under Nero. [Perseus/Tufts primary text](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text.jsp?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.02.0077%3Abook%3D15%3Achapter%3D44) | Written decades later, hostile in tone, and not an eyewitness account of Jesus’ ministry. |
| Acts | Narrates the early Jesus movement from Jerusalem outward and the roles of Peter and Paul. [USCCB Acts introduction](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/acts/0) | It is a Christian theological history and should be compared with Paul’s letters and ancient context. |

**Responsible conclusion:** the combined early Christian and non-Christian source base makes Jesus’ historical existence, association with the early movement, and execution under Pontius Pilate the well-supported historical core. The exact reconstruction of every saying/event and the theological meaning of the resurrection require further argument; the report will not pretend that Tacitus or Josephus independently proves every Christian doctrine.

## Official-document learning path

- **Bible text and notes:** [USCCB Bible](https://bible.usccb.org/bible), a stable official Catholic publication with introductions and notes.
- **How Catholics read Scripture:** [USCCB, Understanding the Bible](https://www.usccb.org/bible/understanding-the-bible).
- **Scripture, tradition, inspiration, and Gospel historicity:** Vatican II, [*Dei Verbum*](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_en.html).
- **Primary non-Christian texts:** Tacitus and Josephus above. Read the passage itself before an apologetics summary.
- **Community verification:** take unresolved doctrinal questions to a qualified pastor/priest and a church Bible study or catechesis program. Record the church/tradition because “official” means different authorities in Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant communities.

# 7. SOURCE AND PRODUCT VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

For every product, paper, video, historical claim, or “doctor says” recommendation:

1. **Define it:** exact ingredient/species/strain/form, intended outcome, population, route, and time horizon. “Magnesium,” “probiotic,” “light,” and “banana water” are not precise interventions.
2. **Find the primary record:** DOI/PubMed/ClinicalTrials.gov for science; FDA/CDC/NIH/EPA/AHA or professional guideline for safety; primary ancient text for history; the relevant church’s own document for doctrine.
3. **Check study type and result:** human randomized result > observational > animal > cells > theory. A protocol has no outcome. A review with mostly tiny biased trials stays weak.
4. **Check fit:** age, health status, training, medication, deficiency, dose/form, comparator, duration, and actual endpoint. Disease-population biomarker effects are indirect for a healthy 26-year-old athlete.
5. **De-duplicate:** the same DOI in multiple folders, articles, or influencer posts counts once.
6. **Separate surrogate from outcome:** LDL, CRP, HRV, microbiome abundance, “detox markers,” and antioxidant capacity are not automatically performance, symptoms, or longevity.
7. **Audit conflicts and certainty:** industry funding is not automatic disqualification, but formulation-specific claims, selective outcomes, high heterogeneity, and publication bias lower confidence.
8. **Verify the product:** FDA does not pre-approve supplements for safety/effectiveness. [FDA supplement regulation](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/fda-101-dietary-supplements) Prefer a live third-party directory such as [NSF Certified for Sport](https://nsfsport-prod.nsf.org/certified-products/index.php), exact lot/model identity, single ingredients, and transparent amounts. A certificate supplied only by the seller is insufficient.
9. **Check current warnings:** FDA warning letters/recalls, interaction databases through a pharmacist, and sport banned-substance rules where applicable.
10. **Predefine the decision:** target metric, trial length, what stays constant, stop signals, and keep/remove threshold. Trial one optional item at a time.

## Animal-evidence gate

Animal data may justify watching an item when there is a plausible human goal, known exposure, reproducible effect across independent laboratories, and adequate toxicology. It still receives **ANIMAL-ONLY / NOT HUMAN-PROVEN**, no personal dose conversion, no stack placement, and no claim that a mouse mechanism will occur in a 230 lb human. Human safety absence is not safety proof. The next action is search for registered/peer-reviewed human trials—not self-experimenting with research chemicals.
